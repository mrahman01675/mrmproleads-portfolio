Category guide

Research-led ABM vs. lead databases vs. intent platforms.

These approaches solve overlapping but different problems. The practical choice depends on whether you need contact coverage, account context, behavioral signals, or human-researched buying context.

Research-led ABM

Account context

Typically combines ICP qualification, company context, relevant signals, stakeholder research and evidence into an account-level handoff.

  • Account-specific research
  • Buying committee context
  • Public evidence trail
  • Human-reviewed interpretation
Lead databases

Contact coverage

Typically optimized for searchable company/contact records, filters, enrichment and list-building workflows.

  • Large contact datasets
  • Firmographic filters
  • Contact discovery/enrichment
  • Self-serve workflows
Intent platforms

Behavioral signals

Typically focuses on account-level activity or intent signals that can help teams identify organizations showing relevant behavior.

  • Intent/behavioral indicators
  • Account identification
  • Scoring or prioritization
  • Platform-led workflows

Where they can complement each other

A sales team may use a database for broad discovery, an intent platform for behavioral signals, and account intelligence research for the smaller set of accounts that need deeper context. MRMProLeads is designed for the research layer: understanding why an account fits, what is changing, who matters and what evidence supports the hypothesis.

Questions to ask before choosing an approach

  1. Do we need breadth or depth?
  2. Do we already have the accounts and need context?
  3. Do we need contact coverage or stakeholder understanding?
  4. What evidence do we need before outreach?
  5. Will a human-reviewed research layer improve the actionability of our existing tools?
