# MRMProLeads — ABM Research Services

**B2B ABM Research & Sales Intelligence for SaaS teams and outbound agencies.**

MRMProLeads helps sales and outbound teams turn target accounts into structured, usable sales intelligence — connecting **account fit, buying signals, decision-makers, evidence, and outreach context**.

The goal is not simply to produce more contacts.

The goal is to make target-account research useful for deciding:

**Why this account → Why now → Who matters → What evidence supports it → How sales can act**

## 1. ICP & Target Account Research

Strong outbound starts before contact discovery.

MRMProLeads can research and structure target accounts against defined criteria such as:

* Industry
* Company type
* Market
* Geography
* Company stage
* Technology environment
* Business model
* Relevant operational characteristics
* Other client-defined ICP requirements

### Typical output

**ICP Definition → Qualification Criteria → Target Accounts → Prioritization Logic**

The objective is to reduce the amount of time sales teams spend researching accounts that were never a strong fit.

---

## 2. Account Intelligence

A company name alone does not explain why an account should be pursued.

Account research can include:

* Company overview
* Products and services
* Target market
* Business model
* Relevant strategic context
* Account-specific rationale
* Observable business developments
* Supporting evidence

### Typical output

A structured account record explaining:

**What the company does → Why it may fit → Why it may matter → What supports the finding**

---

## 3. Buying Signal & Trigger Research

Not every relevant account deserves the same priority.

Research can identify observable signals such as:

* Expansion
* Hiring
* Product launches
* Market activity
* Technology changes
* Leadership changes
* Partnerships
* Funding or investment activity
* Other relevant business developments

Signals are treated carefully.

A visible event does **not automatically mean confirmed buying intent**.

Where intent cannot be confirmed, the research is presented as an **ABM hypothesis**, not a fact.

---

## 4. Decision-Maker & Buying Committee Mapping

B2B purchases rarely involve only one person.

Depending on the account and use case, research may map:

* Executive sponsor
* Economic buyer
* Functional owner
* Technical evaluator
* Operations / RevOps stakeholder
* User champion
* Other relevant buying-committee members

The objective is to understand:

**Who owns the problem → Who evaluates the solution → Who influences the decision → Who may approve the purchase**

This helps sales teams build more relevant account strategies instead of relying on a single generic contact.

---

## 5. Contact Intelligence

Contact research focuses on **relevance and context**, not contact volume.

Where available, research can include:

* Decision-maker name
* Current role/title
* Relevant functional responsibility
* Professional profile
* Public/direct business contact route
* Company contact route
* Third-party sourced contact information
* Source and verification status

### Contact status is kept explicit

Information may be classified as:

* **Public / Direct**
* **Company Route**
* **Third-Party Sourced**
* **Unverified**
* **Verified**

Publicly available information is **not automatically treated as verified or deliverable**.

---

## 6. Technographic Research

Technology can provide useful context for account segmentation and outreach.

Research may cover observable technologies and platforms relevant to the project, such as:

* CRM
* Marketing automation
* Analytics
* Sales engagement
* Customer-support platforms
* Cloud / infrastructure technologies
* Other client-relevant software categories

Technographic findings are documented with appropriate evidence and should not be treated as permanent facts when the underlying technology may change.

---

## 7. Sales Intelligence

The final output should help a salesperson make a better decision.

A sales-intelligence record can connect:

**Account → Fit → Rationale → Trigger → Stakeholders → Evidence → Outreach Context**

Depending on the project, this may include:

* Account prioritization
* Buying-signal summary
* Decision-maker context
* Buying-committee structure
* Personalization angles
* Research notes
* Evidence/source trail
* Confidence level
* Recommended sales use

The purpose is to move from:

**“Here are some contacts.”**

to:

**“Here is why this account matters, who matters inside it, what signals are visible, and what supports the research.”**

# Public-Safe Research

MRMProLeads is designed around evidence-aware and privacy-conscious research.

Public portfolio materials avoid exposing private client information or unnecessary personal contact data.

Where research is published publicly:

* Client identities may be anonymized
* Private contact details are excluded
* Personal information is not presented unnecessarily
* Research findings are separated from assumptions
* Sources and evidence are documented where appropriate

---

# Research Quality Standard

MRMProLeads follows a simple principle:

> **Uncertainty should be visible, not hidden.**

Research distinguishes between:

* Confirmed facts
* Research findings
* ABM hypotheses
* Unverified information
* Conflicting information

The objective is not to make every data point look certain.

The objective is to make the research **useful, transparent, and defensible**.
