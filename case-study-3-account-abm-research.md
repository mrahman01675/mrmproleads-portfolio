## Account 01 — Buy Commercial Energy, Inc.

**Research type:** Public-source account research
**Location:** Glenview, Illinois
**Industry:** Commercial energy / electricity procurement

### 1. Account Fit

Buy Commercial Energy operates around commercial electricity programs, supplier selection, contract negotiations, municipal aggregation, and community solar.

These activities create a clear connection to commercial energy procurement and make the company a relevant example for account-level research.

### 2. Why This Account?

The account was selected because its publicly documented business activities provide a strong basis for researching:

* Commercial energy procurement
* Supplier selection
* Contract-related activity
* Municipal/community energy involvement
* Executive-level ownership

### 3. Trigger / Signal

**Observed signal:**

The company publicly describes commercial electricity programs, supplier selection, contract negotiations, and municipal/community solar activity.

**Important distinction:**
These signals establish business relevance. They do **not** by themselves prove that the company is currently buying a particular product or service.

### 4. Decision-Maker

**Diane J. Elkins — President / CEO**

The research identified a President/CEO-level contact as a potential economic buyer.

**Role classification:** Economic Buyer — research hypothesis

The research also tracked a discrepancy between public professional information and another company-related record rather than silently ignoring it.

### 5. Evidence Standard

The research used an evidence hierarchy:

1. Official company information
2. Government / regulatory sources
3. Municipal / public records
4. LinkedIn and professional context

The account research explicitly separates:

* Observed fact
* ABM hypothesis
* Unverified assumption
* Data discrepancy

### 6. Sales-Useful Output

Instead of returning only a contact record, the research produces:

**Account Fit:** High
**Decision-Maker:** Identified
**Trigger:** Clear
**Contact Route:** Publicly documented
**Personalization Angle:** Municipal/community-energy involvement
**Data Confidence:** High, with documented discrepancy

**Potential outreach direction:**
Lead with the company's commercial procurement and community-energy activity rather than a generic industry pitch.

### 7. Research Quality Principle

The key lesson from this account is:

> Good ABM research is not about collecting more data. It is about reducing unsupported assumptions.

Public-source email documentation also does not equal deliverability verification. Those are separate validation steps.

# Account 02 — Anonymous Enterprise SaaS Example

**Research type:** Anonymized portfolio example
**Client identity:** Not disclosed

### 1. Research Objective

The project focused on understanding enterprise buying dynamics and identifying the people involved in a complex B2B buying process.

### 2. Research Focus

The research structure included:

* Executive sponsors
* Business decision-makers
* Technical stakeholders
* Operational users
* Buying committee relationships
* Engagement recommendations

### 3. Buying Committee Model

A typical enterprise buying committee was structured around:

| Role                | Function          | Decision Context    |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Executive Sponsor   | Leadership        | Strategic decision  |
| Economic Buyer      | Revenue / Finance | Budget approval     |
| Technical Evaluator | IT / Product      | Technical review    |
| User Champion       | Operations        | Adoption / workflow |

### 4. Sales-Useful Output

The deliverable was structured around:

**Account Overview → Buying Committee Map → Role-Based Insights → Engagement Recommendations**

The objective was to give the sales team a clearer view of enterprise buying dynamics rather than simply provide a list of contacts.

### 5. Outcome

**Public-safe outcome:** Improved understanding of enterprise buying dynamics and account strategy.

No confidential client information or unsupported commercial results are disclosed.

# Account 03 — Anonymous Growth-Stage SaaS Example

**Research type:** Anonymized portfolio example
**Client identity:** Not disclosed

### 1. Research Objective

Develop a focused Ideal Customer Profile and prioritize accounts with stronger business relevance.

### 2. Research Work

The research covered:

* Market research
* ICP definition
* Account qualification criteria
* Target-account prioritization
* Account-selection logic

### 3. Account Qualification Logic

The account-selection process was designed to answer:

**Does this company fit the ICP?**

rather than:

**Can we find a contact at this company?**

This distinction helps prevent outbound teams from spending research and outreach effort on poorly matched accounts.

### 4. Sales-Useful Output

The final framework provides:

**ICP → Qualification Criteria → Target Accounts → Prioritization**

The result is a repeatable approach for identifying better-fit enterprise opportunities.

### 5. Outcome

**Public-safe outcome:** Created a repeatable framework for identifying better-fit enterprise opportunities.

No unsupported revenue, meeting, pipeline, or conversion claims are attached to this example.

# Research Quality Rules

MRMProLeads follows several practical research principles:

* Do not present assumptions as facts.
* Separate public information from third-party sourced information.
* Track discrepancies instead of hiding them.
* Do not call an email verified without appropriate evidence.
* Distinguish research hypotheses from confirmed buying intent.
* Protect confidential client and personal information.
* Use evidence to support important account-level claims.
* Treat commercial outcomes as measurable results only after outreach or sales activity has actually occurred.
