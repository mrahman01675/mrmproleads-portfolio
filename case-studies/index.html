ABM Research & Account Intelligence Portfolio

Selected examples are presented at the level of research structure and execution evidence. Private contact details and confidential client data are intentionally excluded.

01 · Enterprise account intelligence

Scope: target-account research, ICP qualification, account context, stakeholder mapping and outreach research across enterprise SaaS accounts.

Output: account-level research notes, buying-role mapping and sales-useful context.

02 · Buying committee mapping

Scope: economic buyer, champion, technical evaluator, influencer and possible blocker mapping.

Output: role-aware account intelligence rather than a single executive contact.

03 · Salesforce ecosystem research

Scope: Salesforce-oriented account research, department mapping, stakeholder roles and account-level qualification.

Output: structured research that connects technology context to the buying group.

04 · ICP & target-account qualification

Scope: ICP definition, market/account criteria, qualification and prioritization.

Output: a focused target-account foundation for outbound research.

05 · Prospect & contact research

Scope: decision-maker identification, role/title research, contact intelligence, source notes and verification status.

Output: cleaner prospect research with uncertainty clearly labeled.

06 · Outreach research framework

Scope: account background, buying signals, role-based messaging inputs and outreach angles.

Output: research organized around the sales decision, not just list volume.

Evidence standard

Public proof separates execution evidence from illustrative samples and internal scale audits. It does not publish personal emails, phone numbers, raw prospect rows or unsupported outcome claims.
