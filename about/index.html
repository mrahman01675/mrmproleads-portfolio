“The goal is not to produce a bigger spreadsheet. It is to give a sales team enough relevant account context to decide who to approach, why now, and who should be involved.”

MRMProLeads was built around research-led ABM, account intelligence, buying committee mapping and sales intelligence for B2B SaaS teams and outbound agencies.

Research philosophy

The operating principle is simple: research the account before treating the contact as the opportunity. Separate ICP fit from buying intent, role from decision influence, and public evidence from assumptions.

Evidence standard

The portfolio prioritizes research quality, business context, human review and responsible handling over raw contact volume. Where public evidence is insufficient, the correct output is a qualification or evidence check—not an invented answer.

Built for

B2B SaaS teams and outbound agencies that need a research layer behind account selection, buying-context discovery and more relevant outreach.
