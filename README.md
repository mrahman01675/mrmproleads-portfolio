## The Research Model

The goal is not simply to produce a list of contacts.

A useful account-research workflow connects the account, the reason for targeting it, the people involved, and the evidence behind the decision.

**Target Account → Account Rationale → Buying Signal → Decision-Maker → Evidence → Sales-Useful Output**

This structure helps sales teams move from:

**“Who can we contact?”**

to:

**“Why this account, why now, and who should we approach?”**

## Research Methodology

A structured MRMProLeads workflow generally follows these stages:

1. **Define the ICP**

   * Clarify company, market, technology, role, and other account criteria.

2. **Research target accounts**

   * Identify and evaluate companies against the target profile.

3. **Investigate account signals**

   * Look for relevant business, growth, technology, hiring, product, market, or other observable signals.

4. **Map decision-makers**

   * Identify relevant executives, functional leaders, and buying-committee members.

5. **Build contact intelligence**

   * Organize available business/contact information with appropriate sourcing and context.

6. **Structure the findings**

   * Turn research into sales-useful intelligence rather than disconnected data points.

## Research Transparency

MRMProLeads follows a simple principle:

**Unverified information should not be presented as confirmed data.**

Where appropriate, research distinguishes between:

* Publicly available information
* Direct/public contact information
* Third-party sourced information
* Research findings and supporting evidence
* Inference versus confirmed information

The objective is to make research useful **without hiding uncertainty behind false precision**.
