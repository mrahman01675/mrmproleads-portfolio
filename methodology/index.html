THE MRMProLEADS METHOD

Four strategic stages from target question to sales action.

These are the four stages clients see: define the target, investigate the account, map the buying group, then activate the research in a sales-ready context.

01

Define

ICP + target criteria. Clarify fit signals, market, company type, technology and account rules.

02

Investigate

Business context + signals. Build structured account context from relevant public company evidence.

03

Map

Buying group. Identify economic, functional, technical, champion and influencer roles where supported.

04

Activate

Why now + outreach context. Turn evidence-backed findings into a practical sales direction.
