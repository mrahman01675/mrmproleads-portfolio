Why this work matters

Lead lists are easy. Account intelligence is harder.

Sales teams rarely need more names. They need enough context to know which accounts deserve attention, who influences the decision, and why an outreach angle makes sense now.

Typical lead list

Contacts without the decision context

  • Large volume, limited account qualification
  • Job titles without buying-role context
  • Little explanation of why the account matters
  • Research scattered across disconnected fields
MRMProLeads approach

Research structured for action

  • ICP-qualified target-account research
  • Economic buyer, champion and evaluator mapping
  • Buying signals and account context
  • Contact intelligence with sourcing notes

Questions

What happens after you request a pilot?

What is the smallest engagement?

A paid 3–5 account pilot. The scope is agreed first so the research depth matches the account set and use case.

Is this a bulk scraped lead list?

No. The positioning is research-led account intelligence: account fit, relevant buying roles, signals, and contact intelligence are organized together.

Do you publish the prospect database?

No. Public proof uses aggregate counts and anonymized examples. Raw names, emails, phone numbers and prospect rows stay out of the public website.

What if some data cannot be verified?

It is not presented as verified. The workflow distinguishes verified/public information from uncertain or unavailable information instead of filling gaps with guesses.
