Questions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use ABM instead of buying lead lists? +

Lead lists contain unverified, often outdated contacts. ABM focuses on accounts that fit your ICP, with human-reviewed buying committee research and personalized messaging, resulting in higher reply rates and better engagement quality.

How fast can I see results? +

Typically, you can expect the first qualified meetings within 2-3 weeks. A full pipeline usually builds over 30-60 days, depending on your ACV and sales cycle.

What makes your approach different? +

I use technographic research (like Salesforce usage) to identify high-fit accounts, map buying committees, and carefully review every profile. No recycled lists, no spam — just precision.

What tools power the research? +

Industry-standard platforms — Apollo.io, Snov.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Hunter.io, NeverBounce, and HubSpot CRM — combined with a 100% human review pass before anything reaches your inbox.
